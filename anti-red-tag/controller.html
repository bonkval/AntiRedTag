Anti Red-Tag

Choose a tab, then press the power button. Only that tab reports visible and focused while you work elsewhere.

The red stop icon immediately disables automatic protection and clears accessible protected pages, including previous tabs. If cleanup is blocked, use the recovery reload icon. Reloading may lose unsaved edits.

Website access is requested when needed. Navigation keeps the same tab selected. Permitted child frames are covered too.

Best effort: websites can use other signals, and browsers can pause background work. Protection may start after a page’s first scripts.
